What it proposes

Method and proposal

This paper proposes the Transformer, a novel network architecture for sequence transduction models. It is based entirely on attention mechanisms, completely dispensing with recurrence and convolutions, which are common in dominant models.

  1. Transformer architecture
  2. attention mechanisms
  3. encoder-decoder configuration

Results & significance

The Transformer model achieved superior quality, greater parallelizability, and significantly reduced training time on machine translation tasks. It established new state-of-the-art BLEU scores and generalized well to other natural language processing tasks.

WMT 2014 English-to-German Translation

The model achieved 28.4 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task, improving over existing best results, including ensembles, by over 2 BLEU.

WMT 2014 English-to-French Translation

On the WMT 2014 English-to-French translation task, the model established a new single-model state-of-the-art BLEU score of 41.8, trained in a small fraction of the time compared to best literature models.
